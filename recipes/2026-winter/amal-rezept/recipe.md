# Fudgy Chocolate Brownies

This is a recipe for Brownies that have a slightly crispy top and a soft Center. I love this recipe because it is easy to make and very tasty :)

**Provided by:** amalramia

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 40 minutes (15 minutes preparation + 25 minutes baking)
- Servings: 9 brownies

## Ingredients
- 150 g unsalted butter
- 180 g dark chocolate
- 150 g sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 90 g all-purpose flour
- 25 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
